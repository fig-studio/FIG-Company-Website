06 / The next partnershipAn invitation to build with FIG

Put your support
where the
building happens.

Help make the next room possible. We’re seeking partners across cloud, infrastructure, databases and developer tools to support future FIG Studio programs.

Start with the outcome that matters to your team. We can shape the format and scope together.

Proposed partnership directions

01

Power the platform.

The full builder journey+

Contribute: Funding, product credits or expertise for the platform that connects registration, team formation, conversations, submissions, judging and post-event discovery.

Shape together: Relevant partner touchpoints and an agreed plan for reporting on participation and project activity.

02

Support the build.

Future hackathons+

Contribute: Event funding, product credits and technical expertise to help teams move from ideas to submissions.

Shape together: A relevant challenge, hands-on support and a post-event summary of participation and submitted work.

03

Meet emerging teams.

Demo days + mixers+

Contribute: Support for a program that brings builders and emerging teams together around their work.

Shape together: A demo showcase, partner participation and introductions with interested, consenting teams.

04

Go deeper.

Private dinners+

Contribute: Support for a focused gathering of founders, researchers and technical leaders.

Shape together: A shared topic, a curated guest profile and space for sustained technical and business conversations.

Future formats and deliverables are proposals. Timing, scope and sponsorship commitments will be agreed with each partner.

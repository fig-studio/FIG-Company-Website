FIG Studio is a Bay Area AI incubator and community.

We bring together designers, engineers, founders, researchers and investors to explore and build what’s next in AI. Through hackathons, makeathons, demo days and mixers, we connect emerging talent with industry practitioners, help turn ideas into working products, and give teams a stage and a path forward.

Frontier

Breakthrough AI capabilities at the technological edge, searching for real-world impact.

Industry

Deep expertise, trusted relationships and customer access earned inside real markets.

Growth

The platform that combines both into enduring, compounding companies.

Four things we hold to, whatever the format

Sponsorship never influences judging or awards.

Judges score the work. Partners can name an award for their own track, not decide the ranking.

Introductions need participants’ consent.

We connect you with teams who have opted in, and we handle the ask.

We report real numbers.

RSVPs are not attendance. A report we promise is one the platform’s data can produce.

The format is shaped together.

Timing, scope, recognition and deliverables are agreed with each partner before anything is announced.

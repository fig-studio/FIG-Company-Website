Who we are

FIG Studio is a Bay Area AI incubator and community.

We bring together designers, engineers, founders, researchers and investors to explore and build what’s next in AI, and give the best teams a stage and a path forward.

Events

Hackathons, demo days and a weekly Open House

Builders, founders, researchers and investors in one room, around real problems and working demos.

Studio

Ten companies built by industry insiders

We partner with experienced operators and technical founders to turn deep expertise into AI-native companies.

Platform

Our own event platform

Registration, AI team matching, submissions and judging in one place. Builders and projects stay visible after the event.

FIG Studio is a Bay Area AI incubator and community bringing together designers, engineers, founders, researchers and investors to explore and build what’s next in AI.

Through hackathons, makeathons, demo days and mixers, we connect emerging talent with industry practitioners, help turn ideas into working products, and give teams a stage and a path forward.

Frontier

Breakthrough AI capabilities at the technological edge, searching for real-world impact.

Industry

Deep expertise, trusted relationships and customer access earned inside real markets.

Growth

The platform that combines both into enduring, compounding companies.

The community is the way in. The studio is the path forward.

Community programs

Where builders meet, build and show their work.

Hackathons and makeathons. Demo days, panels and mixers. FIG Friday Open House, every week in San Mateo. Smaller conversations and private dinners.

Venture studio

Where experienced leaders build AI-native companies.

Ten portfolio companies led by people who know their industries from the inside. Support from venture formation to go-to-market and capital. A founder network that invests in, hires from and sells to itself.

For partners, the community side is what you take part in. The studio side is why standout builders and teams have somewhere to go next, and why the founder network is part of the room.

Three formats

Event partner

From $800

Per event, for teams that want to be in one room: a hackathon, makeathon or demo day.

Logo and mentions before and after. Seats at the demo day and mixer. An office hour, workshop or keynote at higher tiers. A post-event report.

Program partner

[$__ a year]

Across a season of events and the platform, for longer-term collaboration and the most tailored activation.

Lead placement at every event. A challenge track or business scenario. Partner touchpoints on the platform. Quarterly reporting and introductions.

Tool and in-kind partner

Credits

Licences, credits, datasets, compute or hardware for participants. Combines with any format.

Featured in the participant resource guide. A workshop or office hour with your team. A tool usage summary after the event. Credit on the event page and recap.

Four things we hold to, whatever the format

Sponsorship never influences judging or awards.

Judges score the work. Partners can name an award for their own track, not decide the ranking.

Introductions need participants’ consent.

We connect you with teams who have opted in, and we handle the ask.

We report real numbers.

RSVPs are not attendance. A report we promise is one the platform’s data can produce.

The format is shaped together.

Timing, scope, recognition and deliverables are agreed with each partner before anything is announced.
